# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Notifications keep your current menu or unfinished entry visible below them.
- Recording training or no training automatically clears that day's reminder.
- A combined month-end reminder keeps its monthly report action after the day is recorded.
- Built-in reminders remain available in Settings and can be turned off.
- Help now shows the bot version and a What's new screen.
- The public sample report has readable dates and times, a labelled hourly rate,
  column headings and blank unused rows. The private club template is unchanged.

## 0.1.0

Baseline: first public deployment, commit fa3d17a.

- Open registration with separate profiles, settings and training logs for each user.
- Setup and editable profile, IBAN, hourly rates, timezone and gym list.
- One training per day, or an explicit no-training record; editable past dates.
- Guided logging with Back and Cancel, time suggestions and manual time entry.
- Competition entries with a city, and optional saving of a new gym to your list.
- Calendar browsing, marked recorded days, day details and monthly earnings.
- Manual monthly PDF reports using private templates, including overflow pages.
- Configurable evening, month-end, weekly and monthly notifications.
- Notification dismissal buttons and restricted administrator views.
- Automated tests and deployment from main, with a database backup before deployment.
